How the shared list works

Everything you add to Whin Scaur lands on one list that every signed-in teammate sees at the same time, so nobody has to ask what is left to do.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with the account your team gave you.
  2. Open the landing page to see the five newest items.
  3. Finished items show a tick in front of the title.

Good to know

  • The list refreshes every time you open the landing page.
  • Admins and managers sign in at their own pages.
  • Customers sign in at the main sign-in page.

Items older than the newest five stay on the list but are not shown on the landing page.
